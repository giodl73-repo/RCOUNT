# RCOUNT PITFALL Index

RCOUNT tracks pitfalls for reusable election-count evidence, audit replay, and
district aggregation packages. The doctrine keeps RCOUNT useful to BISECT and
other civic systems without letting reusable evidence become legal authority,
consumer policy, or copied sibling-package ownership.

## Integration

- ROLES: `.roles/ROLE.md` reviewers cover count evidence stewardship, audit
  replay, and RPLAN/RCTX/RHIST package bridges.
- VTRACE: RCOUNT does not yet have root `docs/vtrace/`; PITFALL entries cite
  README, family contract, spec, tests, and golden fixtures until VTRACE is
  introduced.
- Tests: Rust tests and verifier commands are evidence for doctrine only when
  they preserve pass/fail meanings, source custody, privacy gates, audit replay,
  and sibling-package boundaries.
